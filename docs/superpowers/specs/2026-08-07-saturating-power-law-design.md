## 1. Goal

Register **`spl` — "Saturating Power Law"**:

```
price   = L / (1 + (t/t₀)^(−β))
log10 p = log10(L) − log10(1 + (t/t₀)^(−β))
```

Early (`t ≪ t₀`), `price ≈ L·(t/t₀)^β` — the Bitcoin power law. Late, it
approaches `L`. At `t = t₀`, price is `L/2`. `t` is years since 2009-07-25.

It is a **logistic in log-time**: substituting `(t/t₀)^(−β) = exp(−β(ln t − ln t₀))`
gives the existing `logi` model with `ln t` for `t`. On a log-log chart, where
the power law is a straight line, `spl` is an S-curve.

## 3. What the data actually say

Rebuilt after R0. This section feeds the Model Info card, so it must not
overstate.

### 3.1 The fit

Under the §4 bounds, **no bound is active** — the optimum is interior:

| | β / slope | t₀ | L | market cap | RMSE |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| power law | 5.0736 | — | — | — | 0.2945 |
| **`spl`** | **5.0910** | **28.31 yr** | **$1.63M/BTC** | **$34.3T** | **0.2945** |
| exponential | — | — | — | — | 0.5538 |

RMSE is quoted to 4 decimals deliberately: the 6-decimal difference
(0.294470 vs 0.294528) implies discrimination the data cannot support (§3.2).

### 3.2 The sample size is ~5, not 5792 — and this governs everything

Measured on the `pl` residuals: **Durbin–Watson 0.0037, lag-1 ρ 0.998127.**
Daily closes of a trending series are not independent observations. The AR(1)
effective sample size is **n_eff ≈ 5.4**.

That number is not an artifact of one formula. The history spans 15.9 yr,
which is **~4.0 four-year halving cycles** — arriving independently at the
same order. *The honest sample size for judging a long-run **shape** is about
four or five Bitcoin cycles.*

Every information criterion changes accordingly:

| | at n=5792 | at n_eff≈5.4 |
|---|---|---|
| ΔAIC vs `pl` | −0.28 | **+2.00** |
| ΔBIC vs `pl` | +6.38 | **+1.69** |

At face value `spl` was a dead heat on AIC; corrected, **both criteria prefer
the plain power law.**

### 3.3 The ceiling is not constrained

Profiling SSE with `t₀` fixed:

| t₀ (yr) | RMSE | market cap | ΔSSE |
|---|---|---|---|
| 20 | 0.295445 | $7T | +3.33134 |
| 25 | 0.294509 | $19T | +0.13524 |
| **28.4** | **0.294470** | **$35T** | **0.00000** |
| 35 | 0.294494 | $98T | +0.08364 |
| 40 | 0.294509 | $193T | +0.13334 |
| 50 | 0.294521 | $595T | +0.17547 |
| 100 | 0.294527 | $19,970T | +0.19703 |
| 1000 | 0.294528 | — | +0.19771 |

Admissible `t₀`, by criterion — **correctly labelled this time**:

| criterion | t₀ (yr) | `pl` |
|---|---|---|
| ΔAIC ≤ 1, n=5792 | 28.4, 35 | outside |
| ΔAIC ≤ 2, n=5792 | 25, 28.4, 35, 40 | outside |
| **ΔAIC ≤ 2, n_eff≈5.4** | **20 … 1000 (all of them)** | **inside** |

An earlier revision reported "$19T–$594T, a 30× range" as a *1-AIC* band. It
was a ΔAIC≤2 band mislabelled, and it assumed n=5801. **Corrected, the data
do not constrain the ceiling at all** — every profiled `t₀` from 20 yr to
1000 yr is admissible, and so is no ceiling whatsoever.

The shape of the surface is still real and worth stating: SSE rises **steeply
below** t₀≈25 (+3.33 at t₀=20) and is **flat above** ≈50, asymptoting to `pl`.
So a very low ceiling fits visibly worse — but "visibly worse" does not
survive the n_eff correction into "excluded". The card must say *consistent
with*, never *excludes*.

### 3.4 What the model claims about the future

Measured against the **fitted `pl`** — the curve users actually see — not
against `spl`'s own asymptote:

| year | `spl` vs fitted `pl` |
|---|---|
| 2020 | **+1.21%** (above) |
| 2024 | −0.80% |
| 2026 | −3.42% |
| 2030 | −13.43% |
| 2038 | −48.75% |
| 2050 | −85.39% |

The deviation is a **pivot**, not a shortfall: `spl` sits *above* the power
law early and below it late. An earlier revision reported "a ~6% shortfall
today", which was both the wrong magnitude and the wrong shape — it compared
`spl` to its own asymptotic power law (β=5.0910), not to the fitted `pl`
(slope 5.0736).

Two further caveats for the card:

- **t₀ = 28.3 yr against data ending at 16.86 yr.** The inflection point is
  extrapolated **1.7× beyond any observation.**
- Log-log curvature is very slightly **convex** (+0.029), the opposite sign
  from saturation — and on autocorrelated data a global quadratic is
  descriptive, not a test.

### 3.5 Linear-time saturation is rejected

`price = L / (1 + exp(−r(t − t₀)))` — saturation on a linear timescale —
scores **RMSE 0.4467, ΔBIC +4832** against `pl`. Decisive even after any
plausible n_eff correction.

Report the ΔBIC, **not** the fitted ceiling: the optimiser drives `L` to its
**lower bound by construction**, so "it pins to the highest observed price" is
an artifact of the bound, not a finding. The rejection stands regardless.

Phrase it as "on this parameterisation, linear-time saturation fits far worse"
— not "saturation is a log-time phenomenon or it is nothing", which
overreaches from a single alternative.

## 4. Fitting

`tools/fit_spl.py`, differential evolution on log10 residuals, `--update`
patching class attrs — the pattern of the existing `fit_*.py` tools.

**Data mask `t >= T_MIN` (1.0)**, matching the constructor and
`fit_shrinking_sigma.py`'s branch. All three must agree; fitting on `t > 0`
optimises a different residual set than `_init_shrinking_bands` derives σ from.

**Parameterisation `(A, β, log10 t₀)`**, `A = log10 L − β·log10 t₀`. `A` and
`β` are well determined; this collapses the degenerate direction onto
`log10 t₀` alone.

| param | bound | mechanism |
|---|---|---|
| `t₀` | [1, 100] yr | box bound on `log10 t₀` |
| `β` | (0.01, 20] | box bound |
| `L` | [max observed price, $1000T cap] | **constraint on a derived quantity** — `L = 10^(A + β·log10 t₀)` is not a coordinate, so DE cannot box-bound it. Implemented as a penalty returning `1e6 + 1e3·(distance outside)` from the objective. |

Verified: under exactly these bounds the fit is **interior on all three**
(β=5.0910, t₀=28.31, L=$34.3T). The $1000T cap does not bind.

**$100T is a display annotation, not a bound** — reached at t₀ ≈ 35.1 yr.

**The fit report must state which bounds are active**, including the derived-`L`
penalty. A pinned parameter is an artifact and must be visible.

**Refit policy: refit all three freely.** No scheduled refit timer exists
(`refit_all_ppl.py` is manual), the cap will not bind, and Phase 2 lets the
user override `L` anyway.

## 5. Model class

`btc_core/_simple.py`, beside its siblings:

```python
class SaturatingPowerLawModel(_ShrinkingBandsMixin):
    name        = "Saturating Power Law"
    short_name  = "spl"           # PERMANENT — in every share link
    legend_name = "SatPL"
    dash_style  = "longdash"      # REQUIRED — mixin supplies none
    quantized   = True

    _log10_L = ...   # patched by tools/fit_spl.py --update
    _t0      = ...
    _beta    = ...

    def __init__(self, price_years, price_prices, quantiles,
                 log10_L=None, t0=None, beta=None):
        # Optional overrides shadow the class attrs. Phase 2 needs a
        # per-request instance with a user-set ceiling; deciding the
        # signature now avoids changing it after the model ships.
        if log10_L is not None: self._log10_L = log10_L
        if t0      is not None: self._t0 = t0
        if beta    is not None: self._beta = beta
        mask = price_years >= T_MIN
        t = price_years[mask]
        residuals = np.log10(price_prices[mask]) - self._model_log10(t)
        self._init_shrinking_bands(t, residuals, quantiles)
        self._build_colors()

    def _model_log10(self, t):
        t_arr = np.asarray(t, float)
        u = -self._beta * (np.log(t_arr) - np.log(self._t0))
        return self._log10_L - np.logaddexp(0.0, u) / np.log(10.0)

    def _build_colors(self):
        """Violet gradient — REQUIRED, the mixin does not define it."""
        self.colors = {}
        n = len(self.quantiles)
        for i, q in enumerate(self.quantiles):
            frac = i / max(n - 1, 1)
            r = int(76 + 60 * frac); g = int(29 + 40 * frac); b = int(149 + 40 * frac)
            self.colors[q] = f"#{r:02x}{g:02x}{b:02x}"
```

`dash_style`, `__init__` (with the `T_MIN` mask) and `_build_colors` were all
missing from the previous revision; each is a **loud** failure —
`AttributeError`, plus `test_models.py::test_all_models_have_dash_style`.

**`logaddexp` is defensive, not load-bearing.** Measured largest intermediate
at the data's left edge: 2.8×10⁷ at the fitted β, 1.8×10²⁹ at the β≤20 bound —
both far inside float64. Overflow starts near β≈210. Keep it as the idiomatic
stable spelling; do not justify it with an overflow claim the numbers refute.

### 6.2 `mi-spl` must be appended LAST — irreversible if wrong

Model Info deep links resolve **positionally**: `_MODEL_INFO_ITEMS[n - 1]`
(`callbacks/routing.py:730`; same pattern in `static_pages.py:501-503`).
Inserting `mi-spl` next to `mi-logi` would shift `mi-s2f` … `mi-citadel` by
one and **silently break every existing `/mi.23`–`/mi.29` and `/9.23`–`/9.29`
link.**

This is the same irreversibility class as `short_name`, and the previous
revision did not mention it.

Required: append `"mi-spl"` **last** in **both** lists, which have different
names and a documented hierarchy —
`layout/model_info/__init__.py:60::_MODEL_INFO_ITEM_IDS` is the **single
source of truth** (so stated at `callbacks/routing.py:535`), and
`callbacks/routing.py:537::_MODEL_INFO_ITEMS` is its hand-maintained mirror.
Then append the AccordionItem last in `_items.py::_build_accordion_items`. The
card therefore sits after `mi-citadel`; that is accepted.

**No test currently guards this three-way sync** (`test_static_pages.py:126`
covers FAQ only). Step 22 adds one.

## 8. Phase 2 — user-set ceiling

**Placement.** Section card in the Tab 1 controls column, revealed when `spl`
is ticked in Display Models — the U₁ mechanism
(`callbacks/user_model.py:127-135`), a clientside callback on `bub-model-show`
toggling `style`. **Verified in R0 that `_DEPRIORITIZED` affects ordering only**
(`layout/display_models.py:212-214`), so `spl` remains a normal checkable
value. Cost: it sits at the bottom of a ~20-row checklist, so the panel is
behind a scroll — a discoverability tradeoff, not a defect.

Not a gear modal: those are full-screen and cover the chart, and the point is
watching the fit degrade while dragging.

**Control.** Log-scale slider in **total market cap** (`L = cap / 21e6`),
marks at $1T / $10T / $35T / $100T / $1000T. Log because the range spans ~2.5
decades, as `bub-yrange` already does in that column.

**Two things Phase 2 must do that are easy to get wrong:**

- **A per-request instance, never a mutated singleton.** `_resolve_model`
  (`figures/common.py:1255-1268`) returns `_app_ctx.PRICE_MODELS.get(key)` —
  a module-level singleton shared by every request in every gunicorn worker.
  Setting `PRICE_MODELS["spl"]._log10_L` from a callback is a **cross-user
  data race**. `u1` is the only key with a per-request escape hatch
  (`UserModel.from_store_dict`, `:1263-1267`); add the same branch for `spl`.
  §5's optional constructor args exist for this.
- **The ceiling must be in the params dict AND in `tab_defaults`.** The params
  dict is the cache key (`cache.py:35`, `sha256(params_json)`): omit it and
  two users with different ceilings share one cached figure; include it but
  omit it from `bubble_defaults()` and the prewarm key stops matching the
  runtime key, costing Tab 1 its first-visit L1 hit. `user_model` does exactly
  this (`tab_defaults.py:506/540/553/564/573`) — mirror it.

**Refit cost.** Constructing a `_ShrinkingBandsMixin` model over the full
history is **1.2–1.8 ms** warm (measured in R0); a 2-parameter local refit
seeded at the optimum is a few ms more. No background-callback machinery
needed. But frame the budget as **one full bubble redraw per mouseup** — the
dominant cost is `build_bubble_figure` plus dispatching a large figure, not
the fit.

`updatemode` is left at its default; `dcc.Slider` already commits on release
and no layout module sets it. It is not a mitigation, just a thing not to
break.

**Live fit-quality readout** (RMSE, ΔBIC vs `pl`) and **Reset to best fit**.
The readout inherits the n_eff problem — it will look ~2 orders of magnitude
too confident — so label it "at face value (n=…)" or show the n_eff-corrected
figure. Qualitatively it still shows the right thing: drag the ceiling low and
the fit visibly collapses.

**Session-only** — a memory `dcc.Store`, like U₁. Not in `_SNAPSHOT_CONTROLS`.
